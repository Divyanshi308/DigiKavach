# DigiKavach - Demo Video Script

**Voiceover:**

"The problem is simple: users have no easy way to verify if a caller, app, or website is legitimate."

**[Screen: DigiKavach logo animation]**

**Voiceover:**
"Introducing DigiKavach - Complete fraud protection for every Indian."

## SCENE 3: Feature Demo - Call Blocking (1:00 - 1:30)

**[Screen: Phone receiving a call]**

**Voiceover:**
"Watch this. A known scam number calls. DigiKavach automatically blocks it."

"The app checks against government databases - DoT MNRL, I4C Registry, RBI fraud lists. If it's a scam, it never reaches you."

"You see the exact risk score and the type of scam."

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Visual Requirements:
- Screen recordings of actual app
- Phone mockups for transitions
- Statistics overlay graphics
- News headline screenshots
- Government database logos (DoT, RBI, I4C)

### Audio Requirements:
- Professional voiceover (Hindi/English)
- Background music (subtle, tech-oriented)
- Sound effects for blocking/alerts

### Tools Needed:
- Screen recording: OBS Studio
- Video editing: DaVinci Resolve (free) or Premiere Pro
- Graphics: Canva or Figma
- Music: Epidemic Sound or Artlist

### File Format:
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Duration: 3 minutes exactly
